## 📈 Featured Project: The Limits of Wealth
**An Econometric Study on GDP and Subjective Well-Being**

This project explores the relationship between socioeconomic factors and national happiness levels, specifically testing the "Easterlin Paradox" and identifying the financial saturation points of human well-being.

### 🛠️ Data & Methodology
- **Dataset:** [World Happiness Report (Kaggle)](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mathurinache/world-happiness-report/data)
- **Scope:** 153 countries analyzing variables such as GDP per capita, Social Support, and Healthy Life Expectancy.
- **Stack:** R (RStudio), Econometric Modeling (GAM, Segmented Regression).

### 🔍 Key Technical Achievements
- **Advanced Modeling:** Implemented **Segmented Regression** to identify specific breakpoints where income growth stops significantly impacting happiness.
- **Non-Linear Analysis:** Utilized **Generalized Additive Models (GAM)** to capture complex, non-linear relationships that traditional linear models miss.
- **Data Integrity:** Conducted rigorous statistical validation including **Residual Analysis** (for heteroscedasticity) and **Normality testing** to ensure the reliability of all insights.

- `/scripts/wh_part1/`: Contains the end-to-end R pipeline (Data Cleaning & Econometric Analysis).
- `/plots/`: High-resolution visualizations (Heatmaps, Regression curves, and GAM outputs).
